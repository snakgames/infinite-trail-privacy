Privacy Policy: The Infinite Trail

Developer: SnakYak · Contact: snakyakgames@gmail.com · Effective date: November 23, 2026

Summary

The Infinite Trail does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What the game stores

Your game progress and settings are saved on your device only. The developer runs no game servers and never receives your save. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's app settings, deletes all of this data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders, for example when an expedition returns. They are scheduled on your device, and nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Internet access

The game does not need an internet connection to play, and it does not send any data about you over the internet.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds advertising, in-app purchases or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The updated policy will name the services used and what they collect. The effective date above shows when the policy last changed.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact snakyakgames@gmail.com.
